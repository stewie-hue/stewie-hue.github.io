About MARAGIA FARM

MARAGIA FARM is a TRFK-certified tea nursery based in Metamaywa Settlement Scheme, near Keroka Town, Nyamira County. We specialize in growing, hardening, and supplying premium tea seedlings and ornamental flowers to farmers and homeowners across Kenya.

Our team will guide you on the best clones for your farm's altitude, soil, and conditions. Contact us for current pricing and availability.
